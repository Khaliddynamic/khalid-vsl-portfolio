03 Process

From footage to finished cut.

A simple working process. Scope, edit, notes, delivery — every step agreed upfront.

01

Brief

Send the footage and a short brief. I confirm scope, ratios, and file specs before starting.

02

Edit

I build the cut around clear pacing and audio, add captions, and refine until the message is easy to follow.

03

Feedback

You review a draft. I apply your notes and tighten any section that doesn't land.

04

Delivery

You receive export-ready files in the formats and ratios your channel needs.
